4. METRIC DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  R2         accuracy in kg/ha. Punishes getting the year LEVEL wrong, which
             LOGO-CV makes structurally unavoidable (model never sees the year).
  R2 de-mean R2 after subtracting the held-out year's mean from BOTH pred and
             actual. Scores the within-year, cross-district pattern only.
  rank r     Spearman r(pred, actual) within the held-out year. Ordering only.

  Why it matters: Nagaland scores R2 = -1.92 but R2_dm = +0.52, rank r = +0.82.
  Good district ranking, wrong level. Reading R2 alone discards that state.

  pooled r        = one Pearson over all district-years in a state
  year-mean r     = correlate yearly MEAN gcvi vs yearly MEAN yield (co-trending)
  within-year r   = de-mean both by year, then correlate (cross-district signal)
  gcvi_gain       = R2(crop) - R2(none)   does GCVI beat ERA5 alone?
  mask_gain       = R2(crop) - R2(raw)    does the mask beat unmasked GCVI?

9. TWO NEGATIVE RESULTS WORTH KEEPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CIRCULARITY. Map-derived entropy CV correlates +0.65 with mask improvement,
  but census-derived entropy gives -0.18 (null). At district level the two
  entropies are uncorrelated (r = -0.009, n = 651), while two independent census
  baskets agree with each other (r = +0.77). The +0.65 is a property of the crop
  map, not of Indian agriculture.

  MAP ACCURACY DOES NOT PREDICT BENEFIT. Assam has the best rice F1 (0.953) and
  low dr. Rajasthan has f1_Rice = 0.000 with 29 training points and the highest
  dr (+1.079). Training-point count vs dr: r = -0.27, p = 0.22.


10. THE ZERO-GCVI EXPERIMENT (tested, reverted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Districts with no pixels under a mask are stored as gcvi_mean = 0. Tested
  dropping those rows from a common panel (removed from all 4 conditions if any
  mask is empty there, to keep the comparison controlled).

  Result: EVERY condition got worse, including none.
    R2 median     keep  0.157 0.229 0.220 0.221
                  drop  0.133 0.131 0.134 0.133

  gcvi_mean = 0 marks "this district grows almost no rice", which is real
  information about its yield -- a rice-presence indicator carried in the GCVI
  column. Dropping it deletes that signal. Zeros are KEPT.
  DROP_ZERO_GCVI = True in logo_cv_all_states.ipynb reproduces the drop run.


11. DATA LIMITATIONS STANDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Crop year 2022-23 (year == 2022) excluded throughout: noisy, and excluding
    it flips Madhya Pradesh from mixed to uniformly positive.
  - Bihar: 10 districts with <5 years of DES yield, south-central absent.
  - n = 27 states -> minimum detectable |r| ~ 0.52. With ~10 predictors,
    P(at least one false positive) ~ 40%. State-level predictor correlations
    are underpowered.
  - Empty Jordi masks: Goa / Andaman / Dadra 100%, Gujarat 70%, Rajasthan 52%,
    Nagaland 46%, Uttarakhand 39%, MP 26%, Maharashtra 22%.
  - Figures compare RF only. Taking the best model per state per condition would
    let model selection appear as a mask effect.


12. DEFENSIBLE CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The crop-type mask reliably improves the CROSS-SECTIONAL correlation between
  peak GCVI and rice yield, but does not improve out-of-sample yield prediction.
  The correlation gain is largely co-trending over time plus a rice-presence
  effect that raw GCVI already captures. The apparent link between mask benefit
  and crop diversity is an artifact of the map itself.

15. THE YIELD-ZEROING BUG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  In gsv_preds_exploration.ipynb cell e31dd4ab and district_level_delta.ipynb
  cell 3:

      merged.loc[merged['gcvi_mean'] == 0, 'yield_kg_ha'] = 0

  Where a mask retains no pixels, YIELD IS OVERWRITTEN WITH ZERO. Those
  district-years become the point (0, 0). Two variables both pinned at zero in
  the same rows correlate strongly for that reason alone.

  Worst cases (stored r_mask vs correctly computed r_mask):
    Nagaland        0.950 -> 0.112   (46% of district-years empty)
    Uttar Pradesh   0.879 -> 0.219   (15%)
    Manipur         0.617 -> 0.299   (25%)
    Rajasthan       0.845 -> 0.542   (52%)
  UP's delta_r flips sign entirely: +0.516 -> -0.144.

  AFFECTED: outputs/gcvi_yield_correlation.csv, state_predictors.csv (delta,
  rel), district_level_delta.csv, and every figure and statement derived from
  pooled delta_r -- including the '+0.44, 23/27 states' headline in section 1.

  NOT AFFECTED: all LOGO-CV results (sections 2, 3, 5-8). build_panel() in
  logo_cv_all_states.ipynb never zeroes yield; it keeps gcvi_mean = 0 as a
  feature value, which is the intended behaviour (section 10). The district-
  level delta of +0.008 is also unaffected in practice: all 116 districts with
  an empty-mask year were excluded by the >=5-year and zero-variance filters,
  so the zeroing never fired on any of the 514 districts analysed.

  RESOLUTION (2026-09-16). The line was REMOVED from all three notebooks
  (gsv_preds_exploration, gsv_preds_exploration_noclass, district_level_delta)
  and replaced with a comment. Decision: keep gcvi_mean = 0 and keep the REAL
  yield; drop no rows. This matches the treatment in logo_cv_all_states.ipynb
  (section 10) -- a zero means "no rice pixels retained", which is a genuine
  rice-presence signal, not a missing value.

  The zero-variance guard is retained: a mask that is empty across an ENTIRE
  state leaves gcvi_mean constant at 0, so r is undefined there.

  AFTER RERUNNING:
    pooled delta_r     +0.438 -> +0.192  (74% of states, p = 0.0061)
    Nagaland  r_mask    0.950 -> 0.112
    Uttar Pradesh delta +0.516 -> -0.144   (sign flip)

    district-level delta  UNCHANGED at +0.0079 (p = 0.344), 514 districts, and
      still zero analysed districts with an empty-mask year -- confirming the

  over all its district-years, then a one-sample t-test on the 27 state values

  per-state p-values in gcvi_yield_correlation.csv must not be used instead.
